## About this policy

Learning App provides English, Marathi, and maths learning activities for primary-school children, including picture matching, money practice, clock practice, and tests. This policy describes the current offline Android app. Parents, guardians, and teachers should review it before children use the app.

## Information processed on your device

The app stores completed test records in its private Android app storage. A record can contain the subject, lesson, completion date and time, time taken, score, question content or image references, answer options, and chosen and correct answers. These records help users review progress and generate detailed reports. Practice activities and unfinished test state may also be retained temporarily or restored by Android.

The app does not ask for a child's name, email, telephone number, school, date of birth, password, or account registration. Results are not assigned to named learner profiles. A test history can still reveal learning activity, particularly if someone links it to a child outside the app.

## Collection and transmission

The current app has no developer-operated server connection, advertising, third-party analytics, or automatic crash-reporting service. We do not receive or sell the test history stored on your device. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, messages, or location.

Android, your app store, or your device provider may independently process installation, diagnostic, or device information under their own settings and policies. This policy does not describe those providers' separate services.

## Android backup and device transfer

The app permits Android to back up and transfer saved test-result preferences. Depending on your Android settings and provider, these records may be copied to a device backup or another device. This is an operating-system feature, not an app-managed cloud account. Temporary PDF reports are not included in the app's configured result-backup scope. Consult your device's backup controls to manage backup, restoration, and deletion of backup copies.

## PDF reports and sharing

When requested, the app creates a PDF in temporary app storage and opens it in the app. Reports contain question illustrations, options, chosen/correct answers, score, date/time, and duration. You may choose to save a separate copy through Android's file picker or share it with an app or recipient you select. The destination may be a cloud storage service. Those providers and recipients control their copies under their own policies.

Parents or teachers should supervise report exports. Do not add identifying information to a report or share children's results publicly without appropriate permission.

## Retention, access, and deletion

Saved results remain until the app's storage is cleared, the app is removed, or another device/backup process changes them. View results in My Test Results and Reports. To delete all locally stored history, use Android Settings > Apps > Learning App > Storage > Clear storage (wording varies). This also resets the app. There is currently no individual-result deletion control.

Exported PDFs must be deleted separately from the file locations, recipient apps, and cloud services where you saved or shared them. Clearing app data does not delete those copies, and Android may restore backed-up records later. We cannot remotely access or delete device-only records or copies held by recipients.

## Children's use and security

Learning App supports supervised learning without requiring a child account. We do not use behavioural advertising or track children across apps. Private app storage and Android's access controls help protect saved records, but anyone with access to an unlocked/shared device may be able to view them. We do not promise absolute security or special app-level encryption of results.
