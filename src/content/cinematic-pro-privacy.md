# Cinematic Pro Privacy Policy

## About this policy

Cinematic Pro is a video-editing app provided by NovaTech Apps ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, work with media, export a project, or contact us.

Privacy contact: developer.novatechapps@gmail.com. Please include "Cinematic Pro privacy" in your subject line so we can identify your request. This policy covers the mobile app. Our [website privacy policy](/privacy-policy/) covers the NovaTech Apps website separately.

## Media, projects and information you provide

- **Media you select:** Video clips, photos, audio tracks and related information, such as file names, formats, sizes, duration and dimensions, are used to open, preview, edit and export the content you choose. Media access is tied to the editing task you request.
- **Editing information:** Project settings, editing choices, preferences and temporary working files may be stored to support the editing workflow and remember your choices.
- **Recordings:** If you use an available capture or voice-recording feature, the camera or microphone input is used for that feature after you grant the required permission.
- **Support messages:** When you contact us, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. Please do not send sensitive media, passwords or other people's information unless necessary to resolve your request and you have authority to share it.

Access to a media file is different from uploading it. A device permission alone does not mean your entire media library is transmitted to us. When you choose to export or share a file, the destination you select determines who receives that copy.

## Android permissions

Cinematic Pro declares the following Android permissions. Access available to the app depends on your Android version, your permission choices and the feature you use. Android may provide a system file picker, selected-item access or a media permission.

- **Photos, videos and audio:** `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` and `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` allow access to the corresponding media types for selecting, previewing and editing your content, subject to Android's access controls.
- **Storage on older Android versions:** `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` is declared for Android 12/12L and earlier, through API level 32. It supports reading media on those versions. This declaration does not grant unrestricted write access to every file on your device.
- **Camera:** `CAMERA` supports photo or video capture when you choose an available capture feature and grant access. Camera hardware is declared optional; a camera is not required for installation.
- **Microphone:** `RECORD_AUDIO` supports recording audio or voice input for an available recording feature after you grant access.
- **Notifications:** `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` supports notifications such as processing or export progress, subject to your notification choices and Android requirements.
- **Foreground media processing:** `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` and `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROCESSING` support an ongoing media-processing task, such as preparing or exporting a video, with the foreground-service behaviour required by Android.
- **Vibration:** `VIBRATE` supports haptic feedback for interface actions or alerts. It does not provide access to your photos, recordings or personal files.
- **Internet:** `INTERNET` permits network connections for online features and any included third-party services described below. Network access does not itself grant access to your media.

You can review and change camera, microphone, media and notification access in Android Settings where Android offers those controls. Some declarations, such as internet, vibration and foreground-service permissions, do not have a separate runtime permission prompt. Denying or limiting access may make the related feature unavailable. You can still manage your files through Android and the storage service where they are saved.

A declared permission identifies an access capability; it does not, on its own, establish that files are uploaded or that a particular advertising or analytics service is installed.

Sensitive permissions must be used for the feature for which access was granted. Optional processing that requires consent is subject to that choice and applicable law.

## Sharing and exports

When you choose a sharing destination or save to a cloud-backed folder, the selected service or recipient receives the file and any information embedded in it. Review the destination and the content before sharing; recipients control their own copies.

Information may also be handled by service providers supporting the features described in this policy, where necessary to operate those features; disclosed where required by law or to protect users and prevent abuse; or transferred as part of a business reorganisation subject to applicable privacy obligations and required notice. Disclosure should be limited to information necessary for the purpose.

We do not sell your personal media. You remain responsible for having permission to edit or share content that includes other people.

## Retention and deletion

Local project files, preferences and temporary editing data remain for as long as needed for your work or until removed by you, app cleanup, clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Clearing app storage can reset preferences and remove unfinished work. Exported media may remain in shared storage, your gallery, a cloud folder or another app after Cinematic Pro is removed; delete those files separately where they are stored.

Android or a storage provider may keep backup or transferred copies according to your device and provider settings. Clearing local app data does not necessarily remove backups, exported files or recipients' copies.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to respond, resolve the issue, maintain relevant support records or meet legal obligations, and is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed for those purposes. Data independently held by third-party providers follows their retention policies and controls.

To request deletion of personal information held by NovaTech Apps, email developer.novatechapps@gmail.com. Identify the app and the information involved without sending unnecessary sensitive material. We may need proportionate information to verify that you are entitled to make the request. We cannot remotely erase device-only files or copies controlled by another recipient or provider.

Contact developer.novatechapps@gmail.com to exercise applicable rights or ask about the information we hold. You may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.
