Aura Music Player ("the app") is an offline music player for Android. It plays audio files that
are already on your device. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on
your device, and what is shared with our advertising partner.

## Summary

- Your music, playlists, listening history and settings stay on your device.
- We don't run servers, require an account, or use analytics or crash-reporting services.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to deliver
  and measure ads, and to personalise them where you've allowed it.
- You can change your ad consent at any time in **Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices**, where
  your region requires it.

## 1. Information that stays on your device

The app processes the following only on your device. We never receive it.

| Information | Why the app uses it |
|---|---|
| Audio files on your device, with their tags (title, artist, album, genre, year), embedded artwork and lyrics files | To build your library and play your music |
| Playlists, liked songs, play counts and listening history, bookmarks, resume positions, queues | To provide library, history, Smart Playlist and resume features |
| Settings, such as equalizer, spatial audio and vibe presets, themes and scan folders | To remember your preferences |
| Search and voice requests ("play … on Aura" from Google Assistant or Android Auto) | To find matching songs in your library; the query is matched locally |

**Smart Playlist** suggestions are produced on your device from your own library and play history.

**Backups:** when you use Settings > Backup and restore, or export an M3U playlist, the file is
saved to a location you choose. The app doesn't upload it anywhere.

**Android backup:** if Android's own backup is turned on, the system may include the app's settings
and database in your Google account backup. You control this in your device settings.

**Home-screen widgets** show the current song and controls. They display information already on
your device and don't send it anywhere.

## 2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, provided by Google LLC or Google
Ireland Limited, depending on your region. Ads appear:

- occasionally, when you switch between library sections;
- in the player, as a clearly labelled ad you can close;
- when you choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock Smart Playlist generation.

To deliver, measure, limit and secure ads, and to personalise them where permitted, the Google
Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID, and app set ID;
- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version;
- ad interactions, such as impressions, taps and video views;
- diagnostic information about ad performance.

This data goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is governed by its own policies:

- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Google advertising policies and controls: https://adssettings.google.com

### Your choices about ads

- **Consent.** In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other regions
  where it's required, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are
  requested. You can review or change your choice in **Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices**.
- **US state privacy rights.** Where applicable, the consent tool also lets you opt out of the
  "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
- **Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads,
  in your device's Google or Privacy settings.
- **Non-personalised ads.** If you decline personalisation, you may still see ads, but they won't
  be based on your interests.

## 3. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Music and audio (`READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`; storage on Android 12 and older) | Find and play audio files on your device |
| Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`) | Show playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen |
| Foreground service and wake lock | Keep music playing in the background and with the screen off |
| Internet and network state | Load and measure ads |
| Advertising ID (`AD_ID`) | Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described in section 2 |

The app doesn't request your contacts, location, camera, microphone, phone or SMS permissions.

## 4. What we don't do

- We don't sell your personal information.
- We don't collect your music library, listening history or files on any server.
- We don't use third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools apart from the Google
  Mobile Ads SDK.
- The app doesn't stream, download or upload music.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

- **On your device:** your data stays until you delete it. Clear the app's storage (Android
  Settings > Apps > Aura Music Player > Storage > Clear storage) or uninstall the app to erase it.
  Backup files you exported stay wherever you saved them.
- **Ad data held by Google:** retained under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID
  and manage Google's ad data through the links in section 2.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws), you may have
rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object
to or opt out of certain processing. We don't hold personal information about you on our systems.
For data processed by Google for advertising, use Google's controls listed above, or contact us
and we'll help where we can.

## 7. Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, or the minimum age required in your country, and we
don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, contact us and we'll help you address it.

## 8. Security

Your library data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app
sandbox. Ad traffic is handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK over encrypted connections.
